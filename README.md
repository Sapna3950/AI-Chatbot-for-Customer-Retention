## 🧑‍💼 Let’s Say a User Says:
👩‍🦰 Gender: Female
👵 Senior Citizen: No (0)
⏳ Tenure: 8 months
📄 Contract: One year
🧾 Paperless Billing: No
💳 Payment Method: Credit card (automatic)
💸 Monthly Charge: ₹4000
## The chatbot translates the user’s words into numerical format because machine learning models can’t read text — they only understand numbers. 🤖
## 🤖 Now, How Does AI Recognize Churn?
Your AI model was trained on past customer data with these exact types of columns.
It learned patterns like this:
## Pattern AI learned	What it means

Tenure < 12 months + High Monthly Charges	=🔥 High chance of churn

Contract = One/Two year + Credit Card Payment =	😊 Less likely to churn

Senior Citizen + Month-to-Month Contract =	⚠️ More likely to churn

Paperless Billing = Yes + Electronic Check	=🚨 Higher risk
